Questions? Answers.

Is it safe for my eyes?

There's no UV. The light is bright, so goggles are included.

How long is a session?

10 to 20 minutes, 3 to 5 times a week. The built-in timer switches it off.

Why are some LEDs dim?

850 nm near-infrared is invisible to the eye. Those LEDs glow faintly at full output.
