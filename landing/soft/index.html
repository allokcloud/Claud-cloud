How it works

Two wavelengths, two depths

Red light is absorbed near the surface. Near-infrared travels further into tissue. Researchers link both to energy production in your cells.

850 nm near-infraredDeeper tissue

FAQ

Good to know

Is it safe for my eyes?

There's no UV. The light is bright, so we include goggles and recommend you don't stare into the panel.

Will it tan or burn?

No. Red and near-infrared light contain no UV.

How often should I use it?

Most routines use 10 to 20 minutes, 3 to 5 times a week.
